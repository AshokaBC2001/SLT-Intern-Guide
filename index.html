Step by step

Your first day at SLT Mobitel

  1. 1

    At the entrance

    Main entrance · Reception
    • Show the email you received from SLT Mobitel at reception.
    • Hand over your ID to reception and take an entry pass.
    • Fill in the Equipment Declaration Form (issued at reception). It gives you permission to take laptops inside.
    No ID with you? Call the Internship Coordinator, Mr. Giridaran.
    071 536 4876 mgiri@slt.com.lk
    Keep the Equipment Declaration Form safe. You must show it every time you enter the SLT Mobitel premises.
  2. 2

    Meet the Internship Coordinator

    Building C · 9th floor Mr. Giridaran
    • Go to the 9th floor of Building C and meet the Internship Coordinator, Mr. Giridaran.
    • Show the email and the reference number you received from SLT Mobitel.
    • Scan the QR codes Mr. Giridaran gives you to join the relevant WhatsApp groups.
  3. 3

    Submit your documents

    Building C · 7th floor HR Trainer Panel
    • Go to the 7th floor of Building C.
    • Hand over the documents SLT Mobitel told you to bring to the HR Trainer Panel.
    • Fill in the documents they give you.
    • Hand the filled documents back to the HR Trainer Panel.
  4. 4

    Collect your placement letter

    Ready in about 3 hours HR Trainer Panel
    • Your placement letter will be ready about 3 hours after you submit the filled documents.
    • Stay inside the SLT premises until then, and collect the placement letter.
    • You will receive these two documents:
    Placement letter
    Internship Program Certificate Request Form
    Keep both documents safe until your internship period ends.
  5. 5

    Get your Trainee ID

    Building C · 9th floor Mr. Giridaran
    • After collecting your placement letter, go back to the 9th floor and meet Mr. Giridaran again.
    • Give him the details he asks for:
    Reference numberNameNIC numberContact numberEmailHome address
    • After you give these details, he will provide your Trainee ID.
  6. 6

    Access Talent Hub and Talent Trail

    About 1 hour after your Trainee ID
    • About 1 hour after you get your Trainee ID, you will have access to Talent Hub and Talent Trail.
    • Download the guide for each system to learn how to use it.
    Talent Hubtalenthub.slt.lk

    Internship management portal for attendance, log book, leave and seat reservation.

    Open Talent Hub Download guide (PDF)
    Talent Trailtalenttrail.slt.lk

    Intern portal for daily worklogs, project tasks, attendance and skill milestones.

    Open Talent Trail Download guide (PDF)
After all six steps

Project assignment meeting

Once you complete these tasks, there is a meeting. The meeting time and location are shared in the WhatsApp group. Attend the meeting and meet Mr. Giridaran and your supervisor. They will assign you to your projects.

During your internship

Important rules

Daily logbook update Every day

You must submit your daily logbook update every day. Failure to submit your daily update may result in your logbook access being terminated.

80% attendance

You must maintain 80% attendance.

Assigned days and supervisor meetings

Come to the SLT premises on your assigned days, take part in supervisor meetings and show your project progress.

Office attire

Wear proper, appropriate office attire whenever you come to the SLT premises.
